Relay baseline—
Baseline value— V
The relay baseline stores the normal voltage difference between the battery (KL30) and the ignition supply (KL15) of this car, caused by wiring and connectors. Set it with the ignition on, the engine off and nothing switching on or off. The device refuses if the ignition voltage is below 8 V, the voltages are not steady, or the difference is larger than 1.5 V. An ignition relay warning is shown when the difference rises more than 0.4 V above the baseline for 2 seconds.
